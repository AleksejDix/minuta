## Legend

- **DD** — day of month, zero-padded
- **MM** — month, zero-padded
- **YYYY** — 4-digit year
- **Order** — segment order: DMY (day-month-year), MDY (month-day-year), YMD (year-month-day)
- Separators vary: `.` (dot), `/` (slash), `-` (dash), `. ` (dot+space), ` ` (space)
- Some locales include trailing literals (e.g. Bulgarian `г.`, Serbian `.`)
